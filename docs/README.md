# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Overview

OctoAcme employs a structured, iterative approach to project management that emphasizes clear ownership, customer value, and continual improvement. Projects begin with a formal initiation phase, where the business need, goals, stakeholders, and resource requirements are documented and validated. Once approved, a detailed planning step creates shippable increments of work with prioritized backlogs, timelines, and acceptance criteria—aligning the team and stakeholders on delivery expectations.

Key personas such as Project Manager (PM), Product Manager (PdM), Developers, QA, and Stakeholders interact through well-defined workflows and communication routines. These include daily standups focused on progress and blockers, weekly syncs, regular demos, and milestone reviews. Project boards are leveraged to track items from backlog to done, with pull requests, code reviews, and automated testing supporting technical quality and transparency at every stage of execution.

Communication routines are proactive and structured: weekly alignment sessions for leadership, frequent delivery team standups, and routine stakeholder updates ensure that status, risks, and next steps are visible. Documentation such as project charters, risk registers, and communication plans are updated regularly and kept centralized in this repository to preserve context and ensure easy onboarding.

Quality assurance is embedded throughout, combining automated (unit, integration, E2E, security scans) and manual approaches with comprehensive checklists for release and deployment. Retrospectives following milestones or incidents yield actionable improvements, which are captured, tracked, and reviewed—driving a culture of learning and process excellence across the organization.
